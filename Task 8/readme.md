## 📚 Research & Notes  

- **What is a VPN?**  
A Virtual Private Network (VPN) encrypts internet traffic and hides your real IP address.  

- **How does a VPN protect privacy?**  
By tunneling data through encrypted connections, preventing ISPs, hackers, and trackers from monitoring activity.  

- **Difference between VPN and Proxy:**  
Proxy hides IP only, VPN hides IP **and** encrypts all traffic.  

- **Encryption in VPN:**  
Most VPNs use **AES-256 encryption** with protocols like **OpenVPN** or **WireGuard**.  

- **Can VPN guarantee complete anonymity?**  
No. VPNs increase privacy but don’t guarantee total anonymity.  

- **VPN Limitations:**  
Free VPNs often have limited servers, slower speeds, and data caps.  

- **Effect on Speed:**  
VPN usually reduces speed slightly due to encryption overhead.
